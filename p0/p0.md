## Resource 1: Open-Meteo Air Quality

**What it provides** (1–2 sentences): Current and forecast air quality for any location in the world by latitude and longitude

**Docs link:** https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api

**Access:** No API key. Cost is free for non commercial use. Limits: fewer than 10,000 calls per day.

**Sample response** (trim it to a few fields; paste the URL you called too):

URL: https://air-quality-api.open-meteo.com/v1/air-quality?latitude=0.51&longitude=101.45&current=pm2_5%2Cus_aqi

```json
{
  "latitude": 0.5,
  "longitude": 101.5,
  "timezone": "GMT",
  "current_units": { "pm2_5": "μg/m³", "us_aqi": "USAQI" },
  "current": {
    "time": "2026-10-29T02:00",
    "pm2_5": 9.0,
    "us_aqi": 56
  }
}
```

**Who could this data help, and with what?** (2–3 ideas; name real people and a real need, e.g. "first-year students who want to ...")

1. Indonesian citizens who want to check accuracy of daily air conditions affected by the Wildfires
2. Residents of Riau who want independent air quality reading to compare against what local officials announce
3. Parents in Riau who need to decide each morning whether it's safe to let their children play outdoors

**One risk** (missing data, bias, privacy, or reliability):
values are estimates from an atmospheric model, not readings from a physical sensor on the ground, each point covers a wide area. So it can miss thick smoke right next to a fire, or differ from local monitors.

## Resource 2: TheMealDB

**What it provides** (1–2 sentences): An open crowd-sourced database of recipes from around the world

**Docs link:** https://www.themealdb.com/api.php

**Access:** a free public test key ("1") goes in the URL, for development and educational use. Cost is free (a $10 lifetime supporter key to release an app publicly). Rate limits: no published numbers

**Sample response** (trim it to a few fields; paste the URL you called too):

URL: https://www.themealdb.com/api/json/v1/1/filter.php?i=salmon

```json
{
  "meals": [
    {
      "strMeal": "Baked salmon with fennel & tomatoes",
      "strMealThumb": "https://www.themealdb.com/images/media/meals/1548772327.jpg",
      "idMeal": "52959",
      "strArea": "British",
      "strCountry": "United Kingdom"
    },
    {
      "strMeal": "Fish Soup (Ukha)",
      "strMealThumb": "https://www.themealdb.com/images/media/meals/7n8su21699013057.jpg",
      "idMeal": "53079",
      "strArea": "Russian",
      "strCountry": "Russia"
    }
  ]
}
```

**Who could this data help, and with what?** (2–3 ideas)

1. College students who buys humongous fillets of salmon from Costco
2. Busy students/workers who meal prep to have balanced meals amidst pack schedules
3. International students who miss home food and want to browse recipes by cuisine

**One risk** (missing data, bias, privacy, or reliability):
There's also no nutrition or calorie data, so meal preppers who track nutrition would need another source
